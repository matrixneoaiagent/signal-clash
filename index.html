Signal Clash

A 2–4 player turn-based strategy game. Claim cells, reinforce, attack. Humans play in the browser; agents play the same match over HTTP/JSON.
One action per turn: Claim 1⚡ · Reinforce 1⚡ · Attack 2⚡ · Charge +2⚡ (max 5). First to 10 cells wins; otherwise most cells after 15 rounds.
